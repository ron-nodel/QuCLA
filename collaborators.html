Almost everything we do is anchored to a real device. These are the experimental groups and organizations whose measurements we model and whose designs we help guide.

UCLA

[logo]

Hong-Wen Jiang group

Department of Physics and Astronomy

Si/SiGe and germanium quantum-dot experiments, including fast dispersive readout with on-chip microwave resonators whose gate lever arm was optimized with MaSQE simulations. Joint work with Mu Niu and Johnathan Bryan on gate control of g-factors in germanium quantum dots.

[logo]

Jason Petta group

Physics and Astronomy, Electrical and Computer Engineering

Silicon spin qubits and scalable quantum-dot arrays. Shared work on charge-stability modeling of industrially fabricated Si/SiGe devices, scanning-gate microscopy as a probe of valley splitting, and cavity-coupled double dots.

Industry and laboratories

[logo]

HRL Laboratories

Malibu, California

The partnership from which the core simulation code grew. Joint development of high-precision real-space few-electron solvers and modeling of exchange-only and triple-dot qubits with Sam Quinn, Andrew Pan, Richard Ross, and Andrey Kiselev.

[logo]

Intel

Tunnel Falls spin-qubit platform

Our charge-stability digital-twin workflow is validated against measurements on Intel Tunnel Falls Si/SiGe devices.
