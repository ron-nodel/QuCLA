Almost everything we do is anchored to a real device. These are the experimental groups and organizations whose measurements we model and whose designs we help guide.

UCLA

[logo]

Jason Petta group

Physics and Astronomy, Electrical and Computer Engineering

Scanning-gate probe design and dot shuttling. Working with Efe Cakar, Harry Levinson, Avery Jackman, and Gordian Fuchs (Princeton).

[logo]

Hong-Wen Jiang group

Department of Physics and Astronomy

Using MaSQE to explore strain effects in germanium quantum dots. Working with Johnathan Bryan and Alex Galkin.

[logo]

Peihong Zhang

UCLA, with Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

Density functional theory for valley splitting in Si/SiGe heterostructures, with Vince Lordi and Joel Varley (LLNL) on the MQC projects.

Charge noise identification and coupling to valley physics. Detuning-axis pulsed spectroscopy of pO qubits on Intel Tunnel Falls devices.
